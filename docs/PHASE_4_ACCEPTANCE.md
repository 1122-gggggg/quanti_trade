# Phase 4 Acceptance Criteria

- Paper orders are processed by a durable execution service with an account ledger.
- Broker adapters are isolated, observable and idempotent.
- Secrets never reach the browser or strategy runtime.
- Live trading requires explicit enablement and enforceable risk limits.
- Licensed real-time, tick, order-book, fundamentals and news data are entitlement-controlled.
- All order state transitions and external acknowledgements are auditable.
